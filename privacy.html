Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Techsavvy Tanzania ("we", "us") runs this website to help young people learn technology skills and join our community. This page explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, who else handles it, and the choices you have. We try to keep it simple and to collect only what we need.

1. What we collect

  • When you apply (Join Us): your name, email address, an optional phone number, the type of application you choose, and the answers you give on the form, such as interests, goals, skills, experience, links to your portfolio, organization details or a proposal.
  • When you sign in: we use Google sign-in. Google shares basic profile information with us, such as your name, email address and profile picture. We never receive your Google password.
  • When you use courses: which courses you enroll in, and when.
  • Our own records: the status of your application (for example Pending or Approved) and internal notes our team may add while reviewing it.
  • On your device: your sign-in is kept in your browser so you stay signed in. We do not run advertising or analytics trackers on this website.

2. How we use it

  • To review your application, contact you about it, and let you follow its status.
  • To let approved learners enroll in and follow courses.
  • To run our programs, projects and community, and to keep the website secure.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who else handles your information

  • Supabase stores our database and handles sign-in. Our project is hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt), so your information is stored outside Tanzania.
  • Google provides Google sign-in, and Google Fonts, which your browser loads to display text.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages) hosts the website files.
  • jsDelivr delivers a code library that our pages use.
  • Resend may be used to send automated emails to you.
  • WhatsApp and email: when we contact you this way, those services handle the message under their own policies.

These services can see technical details, such as your IP address, when your browser connects to them. We may also share information if the law requires it.

4. Your Application Number

Anyone who has your Application Number can see your name, the type of application and its status on the tracking page. Please keep it private.

5. Who can see your information

Only authorised members of our team can view applications. You can see your own applications and course enrollments when you are signed in.

6. How long we keep it

We keep application and account information for as long as it is needed to run our programs and support our community. We delete it when you ask us to, unless we are required to keep it by law.

7. Your choices

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Email us at techsavvymanagment@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. You can sign out at any time, and clearing your browser data removes the saved sign-in.

8. Young people

Our programs are for young people. If you are under 18, please use this website and apply only with the knowledge of a parent or guardian.

9. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information: connections to this website use HTTPS, and database rules limit who can read applications. No system is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

10. Changes to this page

We may update this policy as the website grows. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

11. Contact

Questions about your information? Write to techsavvymanagment@gmail.com.
