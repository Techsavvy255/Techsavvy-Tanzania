# TECHSAVVY TANZANIA

**Empowering Youth Through Technology, Innovation, and Digital Skills.**

## About Us

Techsavvy Tanzania is a youth-driven technology initiative focused on empowering young people through technology, innovation, digital skills, and practical digital solutions.

We aim to create opportunities for learning, collaboration, innovation, and participation in the digital economy.

## Our Focus Areas

- Web Development
- Mobile Application Development
- Software and Digital Product Development
- Artificial Intelligence and Digital Innovation
- Networking and Cybersecurity Fundamentals
- Graphic Design and Digital Marketing
- Technology Education and Community Development
