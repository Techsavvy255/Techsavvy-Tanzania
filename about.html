About Us

Who We Are

Techsavvy Tanzania is a technology platform helping young people and communities learn, build, and create economic opportunity through practical technology skills.

Our Story

Techsavvy Tanzania was founded to close the gap between young Tanzanians and the practical skills needed to build careers in technology. Instead of only teaching people to use computers, we focus on teaching people to build with them — through hands-on training, real projects, and a supportive community.

What started as a small group with a big mission has grown into a platform covering Artificial Intelligence, Web Development, Design, Cybersecurity, Networking, Digital Marketing, and Content Creation.

Mission

Empowering young people through technology education, practical skills, innovation, and digital opportunities.

Vision

Building a generation of skilled, creative young people able to use technology to drive real change.
