Project Case Study In Development

DaLink

An e-commerce application connecting buyers and sellers through a simple, accessible digital marketplace.

The Problem

Many small sellers struggle to reach buyers online without relying on expensive or complicated platforms. DaLink aims to give sellers and buyers a simpler, more direct way to connect and transact digitally.

The Solution

DaLink is being built as an e-commerce application where sellers can list products and buyers can browse and purchase — designed with simplicity and accessibility as core priorities.
